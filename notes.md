**WAKEFIRE, Chapter One: The Sleeping God**

The god of the sky fell asleep, and the city of Ashkel went with him. Azar, the night watchman, is the only one still awake. Nod off to cross into the dream, wake the people who matter, and climb the ziggurat to find out why the god won't wake up.

**New in 1.2.0:** the city has been rebuilt to match its people. Every house, wall, market stall, tree and jar is now a 3D model drawn into the pixel picture, lit by the moon, throwing its own shadow. Ashkel has real streets, a night market and a glazed Temple Gate; the ground has relief on every map; fires light the walls that face them and leave the roofs in moonlight; the sea moves.

**From 1.1.0:** every character is a 3D figure rendered into the pixel picture, with real motion: a run with weight and momentum, a three-cut combo, a roll in any direction, a cape and lantern that swing, and animals that walk on planted feet.

- **Android:** download `WAKEFIRE.apk` on your phone and open it. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser or files app.
- **Google Play:** `WAKEFIRE-Play.aab` is the bundle for the Play Console.
- **iPhone and iPad:** unzip `WAKEFIRE-iOS-Xcode.zip`, open `Unity-iPhone.xcodeproj` in Xcode, choose your team under Signing, and run it.
- **Web:** play in your browser at https://neomaverick1.github.io/wakefire

Controls: on a phone, put your thumb down anywhere on the left to walk; the blade button sits under your right thumb with roll, talk, the Wakefire and nod off around it. On a computer, use WASD or the arrows to move, J or Space for the khopesh, K to roll, E to talk, F for the Wakefire and Q to nod off. Gamepads work too.

The game saves every time you walk into a new place and every time you find something.
